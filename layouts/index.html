New number. Same crew.

FTC Team 35817, The Cosmic Microwave, is officially a rookie team, but our core members are seasoned competitors. We previously competed as FTC 27393 “The FBI — FIRST Bot Inventors” at North Tapps Middle School.

Today we are seven students, a mix of two-year veterans and rookies, at Auburn Riverside and Bellarmine Prep. The build philosophy carried over with us.
